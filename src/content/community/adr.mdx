<Intro>

An ADR records a decision that was hard to make and would be expensive to
reverse — what was decided, what it costs, and what would change it. These seven
are the load-bearing ones: almost every surprising thing about skyl's API traces
back to one of them.

</Intro>

## Why keep them

A decision without a recorded reason gets re-litigated every six months, and
eventually gets reversed by someone who did not know what it cost to make.

Three of these seven are the reason a reader is surprised by something. If you
have ever wondered why Claude needs a second `go get`, why skyl ships no model
constants, or why there is no `provider/copilot` — the answer is here rather
than in a commit message from a year ago.

## Format

Each follows the same shape: **Context**, **Decision**, **Consequences** (good
and bad), and **Alternatives considered**. The alternatives section is the one
worth reading — it is where you find out whether the obvious idea was missed or
rejected.
